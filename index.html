About the Mercator Projection

Drag the map to explore how the Mercator projection distorts areas near the poles. Notice how Greenland appears much larger than it actually is compared to countries near the equator!

Use mouse wheel to zoom in/out. The grid lines show the distortion effect more clearly at different zoom levels.

Key observations:

  • Areas near the poles (like Greenland, Antarctica) appear drastically oversized
  • Equatorial regions maintain more accurate relative sizes
  • The grid spacing increases dramatically toward the poles
  • Angles and directions are preserved (conformal projection)
