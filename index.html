Interactive Mercator Projection Center

Click and drag anywhere on the map to move the projection center to that location. Watch how the distortion changes dramatically as you move the center point!

Use mouse wheel to zoom in/out. The red crosshair shows the current projection center.

Key observations when changing the center:

  • Areas near the new center appear more accurate in size
  • Areas far from the center become increasingly distorted
  • The grid lines spread out as you move away from the center
  • Countries at the "poles" of your projection become enormous
  • The equator line (red) shows the line of least distortion

Try centering on: Greenland, Australia, your hometown, or the North Pole to see dramatic changes!
